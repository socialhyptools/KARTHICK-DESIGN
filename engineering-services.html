Our Capabilities

Engineering Services We Offer

Structural Design

Expert structural design and consultation for MS gates, sheds, railings, and custom fabrication works. We produce detailed engineering drawings that meet local building codes and site requirements.

Site Survey & Measurement

Thorough on-site surveys and precise measurements to ensure every fabricated component fits perfectly. We assess ground conditions, load requirements, and installation constraints before production begins.

Project Management

End-to-end project management from quotation to handover. We coordinate fabrication schedules, material procurement, quality checks, and on-site installation to deliver on time and within budget.

Where We Apply It

Engineering Applications

Industrial Shed Design

Structural drawings and engineering calculations for pre-engineered sheds, factory roofing, and large-span roof structures.

Gate & Railing Layout

Site-specific design and measurement for compound gates, staircase railings, balcony guards, and access control structures.

Mezzanine Floor Planning

Load analysis, column placement, and structural drawings for mezzanine floors, storage platforms, and elevated walkways.

Parking Canopy Engineering

Engineering design for MS parking canopies, carports, and shade structures with wind and rain load calculations.

Renovation & Retrofitting

Assessment and engineering plans for upgrading, reinforcing, or modifying existing MS structures to meet new requirements.

Maintenance Contracts

Scheduled inspection, lubrication, coating, and repair services to extend the life of fabricated MS structures and prevent failures.
